# Kitchen United

> Kitchen United was a Pasadena, California ghost-kitchen operator that ran shared
> commissary kitchens ("Kitchen United Mix") where multiple restaurant brands cooked
> for delivery and pickup from one location, including in-store food halls with Kroger.
> The company wound down its physical footprint in late 2023 / early 2024, and on
> 2024-03-14 sbe/C3 founder Sam Nazarian acquired its IP and ghost-kitchen assets into
> the Everybody Eats holding company alongside C3 and Nextbite. Kitchen United is
> defunct and never published a public developer API.
